Privacy

Last updated 18 November 2026

Candor Works LLC runs this site. It uses Google Analytics to count visits and to see how the site is used, and Leadfeeder to see which companies visit.

Both store cookies in your browser. Google Analytics records the pages you view, what you click, how you arrived, your device and browser, and your approximate location. Leadfeeder works out which company a visit comes from, from the network the visitor is on. Neither gives us your name or your email address.

We do not use this for advertising, and we do not sell information about visitors.

To opt out of both, turn counting off for this browser. Google also offers its own opt-out add-on and explains how it uses information from sites that use its services; Leadfeeder publishes its privacy information.
